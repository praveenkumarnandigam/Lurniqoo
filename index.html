Introduction

Lurniqoo is a student-focused learning platform designed to simplify access to syllabi, study materials, and structured academic resources. It helps university students quickly find what they need without confusion or delay.

By organizing content by department, year, semester, and subject, Lurniqoo brings clarity to the academic journey and reduces the stress of searching across scattered platforms.

Vision & Purpose

Accessibility

Academic resources available anytime, anywhere, without complex processes.

Organization

Materials structured by department, year, semester, and subject.

Growth

Continuously evolves with notes, features, and tools to support students.

Platform Features

  • Structured academic navigation
  • Centralized syllabus and resources
  • Fast, lightweight performance
  • Clean and distraction-free interface
  • Secure document viewing

Learning & Notes System

Lurniqoo is expanding into a full learning environment with structured academic notes designed for concept clarity and exam preparation. Notes will be organized by subject and unit, helping students study efficiently and confidently.
